# Privacy Policy

**Bot:** Nova Brand Hub Helper (the "Bot")
**Operated by:** Nova Brand ("we", "us")
**Contact:** josh@jaminiostudios.com
**Last updated:** 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bot collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what choices you have. By using the Bot (for example by pressing an application button and answering its questions) you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the Bot.

## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot runs department applications inside Discord servers. A person presses a button, answers questions in a direct message (DM) with the Bot, and the answers go to that server's staff, who review them. Server administrators can also use the Bot's management commands (for example for roles, channels and templates). Those commands act on a server's own settings and are restricted to people the operator has approved.

## 2. Information we collect

| Information | Examples | When |
|---|---|---|
| Discord identifiers | Your user ID, username, display name, the server ID | When you apply or use a command |
| Server details | When your Discord account was created, when you joined the server, up to 10 of your role IDs in that server | When you submit an application, so reviewers have context |
| Your application answers | Which group or department you chose, your Roblox username, why you want the role, your experience, your timezone, hours per day you can be active, days you can be active | What you type during the application |
| Roblox information | Your Roblox user ID and account creation date, found from the username you gave | When you enter a Roblox username |
| Application progress | Answers entered so far in an unfinished application | While you answer, so a restart doesn't lose your place |
| Staff activity on your application | The reviewer's ID, the decision and reason, follow-up questions and your replies, a hold message, private staff notes, notes about brief or similar answers, and timestamps | When staff review it |
| Server settings | The review channel, log channel, reviewer role, Intern role, the lottery role, whether the economy is on and where it can be used, the application panel's message IDs, and which departments are open or closed | When a server administrator sets the Bot up |
| Server activity (only if a server's administrators turn it on) | Who joined or left, nickname changes, which roles were added or removed from whom, changes to channels, roles and server settings, and bans, kicks and timeouts, each with the member's Discord name and ID and, where Discord shows it, who did it | As it happens, and posted straight into the log channels that the server's administrators chose. The Bot does **not** store these events. Only the choice of log channels is stored |
| NovaBux economy (only if a server turns it on) | Your Discord user ID and, for each server you play in, your balance, bank, items, cooldown times, daily streak, recent trading results and any lottery tickets you hold | When you use an economy command. NovaBux is a game currency with no real-world value and can't be bought or cashed out |
| Intern invitation (only if a server's reviewers use it) | Which staff member invited you, the department, when it was sent, and whether you agreed to the staff terms or declined, with the date | When a reviewer sends you an invitation and when you answer it |
| Weekly question (only if a server turns it on) | The text of questions that staff queue, the name and ID of the staff member who submitted each, the channel and thread of the current and recent questions, and the chosen channel and roles | When staff use the question commands. Questions are posted publicly in the server |

**Your timezone:** if you type a town or city, we use it only to work out your timezone. We keep **only the timezone** (for example "Europe/London (UTC+0)"), not the place you typed.

## 3. What we do **not** collect

- We do not read the messages people send in server channels. The Bot does not request the "Message Content" permission. This includes the server activity logs, which record events such as joins and role changes, never message text.
- We read your DM messages to the Bot **only** while you are answering an application.
- We do not collect payment details, passwords, your email address, or your location.
- We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## 4. How we use it

- To run the application you chose and show it to the server's reviewers.
- To tell you the outcome, ask you a follow-up question, or let you check or withdraw your application.
- To enforce rules such as waiting before reapplying after a denial, and to flag answers that look copied.
- To keep a record of decisions so staff can do their job, and to keep the Bot working and secure.
- To produce anonymous statistics for the server's reviewers, such as how many applications there are for each department and how long decisions take.
- If a server's administrators turn on activity logs, to record joins, leaves, role changes, server changes and moderation actions for that server's staff.

## 5. Who can see it

- **The server's reviewers and administrators** can see your application, including your answers, your Discord and Roblox details, and the staff notes. Your answers may also be recorded in the server's review channel and log channel, which that server controls.
- **Us**, as the operator, can access the stored data in order to run and support the Bot.
- **You** can see the status of your own applications with `/status`.

Servers are separate communities. A server's staff decide who may review applications there, and we are not responsible for how they handle information once they can see it.

## 6. Other services we use

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Discord** | Everything you send through Discord | The Bot runs on Discord. See Discord's privacy policy. |
| **Roblox** (public lookup) | The Roblox username you enter | To check the account exists and show you its avatar for confirmation. |
| **Open-Meteo** (public geocoding) | The place name you type, if it isn't a major city or abbreviation | To work out your timezone. No account or other detail is sent. |
| **Our hosting provider** (one.com) | The Bot's stored data | The Bot and its data are hosted on a server there. |

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Applications waiting for a decision, or on hold | Until a decision is made or you revoke it |
| Decided or revoked applications | Your answers and details are deleted **2 years** (730 days) after the decision |
| A short record of a denial (no answers) | Until the reapply waiting period ends (14 days by default) |
| Anonymous statistics (the department, group, outcome and dates of an application, with **no names, IDs or answers**) | Kept indefinitely, so we can report how many applications there have been and how long they take |
| Unfinished application progress | Up to 24 hours |
| NovaBux accounts | Until you delete them with `/my-data delete`, a server's staff reset them, or the Bot is removed from that server. Deleting an account loses the balance, items and any lottery tickets |
| Intern invitations | Unanswered, declined and expired ones are deleted after 30 days. The record that you agreed to the staff terms is kept until you delete it with `/my-data delete` or the Bot is removed from the server |
| Queued and recent weekly questions | Queued ones until posted or removed; the last 60 posted ones are listed, and a server can have the Bot delete old question threads automatically |
| Server settings and everything stored for a server | Deleted automatically when the Bot is removed from that server (a nightly backup can keep a copy for up to about 30 more days) |
| Nightly backups of the stored data | The most recent 30 backups, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to about 30 more days |

The retention periods are our defaults and a server's setup can change them. Copies in a server's own review or log channel are kept for as long as that server keeps them, and we cannot delete messages in your DMs.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **Withdraw:** use `/revoke` to withdraw a pending application at any time.
- **See your applications:** use `/status`.
- **See everything the Bot holds about you:** use `/my-data view`.
- **Delete it yourself:** use `/my-data delete` (you confirm first). This removes your applications, including the review message and any decision message the Bot sent you, your unfinished application, and your name on weekly questions you submitted. A pending application is withdrawn, Intern invitations (including the record that you agreed to the staff terms) are removed, and so are your NovaBux accounts. A short record of a denial (no answers) stays until the reapply waiting period ends. Anonymous statistics are not linked to you, so they stay.
- **Ask us to access, correct or delete your data:** contact us at josh@jaminiostudios.com. We will respond within one month. Deletion removes the data we hold, but we cannot remove copies held by a server's staff or in Discord itself.
- Under UK data protection law you also have the right to object to how your information is used, to restrict it, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## 9. Why we are allowed to use your information (UK)

Nova Brand is the controller of the information the Bot stores. We use it because it is needed to run the application you asked to make (contract), and for our legitimate interests in running the Bot securely and keeping a record of staff decisions. Where we rely on your agreement (for example the Intern terms), you can withdraw it by deleting your data.

## 10. Age

You must be **at least 13** (or the minimum age to use Discord in your country, if higher). The Bot is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Bot, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 11. Security

We limit who can access the server, use key-based logins, restrict the Bot's commands to approved people, and keep secrets out of the stored data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version, and continuing to use the Bot after a change means you accept it.
